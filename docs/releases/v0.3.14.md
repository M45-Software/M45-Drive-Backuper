# v0.3.14 — Control active and upcoming backup jobs

- Add contextual Pause/Resume and Cancel actions in the tray and main window for backups and integrity checks.
- Offer 1-, 4-, and 24-hour postponements with a visible due time, processed even with the monitor closed.
- Pause future automatic starts without changing saved schedules, and offer a one-day reminder snooze.
- Freeze the complete service process group on cgroup v2; keep cancellation available for paused jobs and queued checks.
- Reject stale job/plan requests, preserve check selection across deliberate pauses, and avoid resuming replaced or reboot-interrupted processes.
- Identify user-cancelled runs and keep incomplete results separate from successful runs.
